Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Earnova. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our platform for tasks, spinning the wheel, and earning rewards.

2. What Information We Collect

  • Profile Data: When you log in (e.g., using Google), we securely collect your Name and Email Address to create and maintain your account.
  • Payment Details: To process your withdrawals, we collect your chosen payment method details, such as UPI ID, Bank Account Number, and IFSC Code.
  • Activity Data: We track your in-app activities, including completed tasks, daily check-in streaks, spin history, and your current wallet balance.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To set up, maintain, and secure your Earnova account.
  • To calculate your points, track your tasks, and ensure accurate wallet balances.
  • To securely process your withdrawal requests (UPI/Bank transfers).
  • To prevent fraud, multiple fake accounts, and ensure fair play for all users.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Security & Database: We use Google Firebase for highly secure authentication and data storage.
  • Advertisements: We use Google AdSense to display ads. AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your internet browsing history.
  • Task Partners: Our earning tasks redirect you to third-party apps. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Once you leave Earnova, their respective privacy policies apply.

5. Data Security

Your wallet balance and personal details are stored securely using industry-standard databases. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or payment details with any unauthorized third parties.
