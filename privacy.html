Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Earnova. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard it when you use Earnova for partner tasks, daily rewards, and wallet features.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: When you sign in, we collect basic profile details such as your name and email address to manage your account.
  • Payout Information: When you request a withdrawal, we collect your chosen payout details (such as UPI ID or bank details) solely to process verified reward payouts.
  • Activity & Task Data: We record completed tasks, daily check-ins/rewards, referral activity, and wallet balance updates so your rewards remain accurate.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain your Earnova account.
  • To track task completions and credit eligible rewards to your wallet.
  • To review and process UPI or bank withdrawal requests.
  • To detect spam, duplicate accounts, or policy abuse and keep the platform fair for all users.

4. Partner Offers & Third-Party Links

Earnova displays partner offers and external links provided by affiliate and reward partners. When you click a partner offer, you may be redirected to an external website or app governed by that partner's own privacy policy and terms. Reward confirmation depends on valid partner tracking and verification.

5. Data Security & Retention

We use standard cloud infrastructure and access controls to protect account and wallet records. Data is retained only as long as necessary to operate your account, fulfill reward/payout records, or meet legal requirements.

6. Contact & Support

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your account data, please reach out through the Help & Support section inside Earnova.
