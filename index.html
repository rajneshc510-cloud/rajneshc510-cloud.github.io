Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any joining or registration fee on Earnova? ▾

No. Joining Earnova and browsing offers is completely free. Some partner offers (such as specific Demat accounts) may have their own trading or minimum balance requirements, which are clearly listed before you start.

How long does verification and withdrawal take? ▾

Partner verification depends on successful KYC/task completion on the partner platform. Once your reward is available in your wallet, UPI and Bank withdrawal requests (minimum ₹30) are typically processed within 24 hours.

Why does an offer show as "Started ✓"? ▾

Each partner offer can only be rewarded once per user account. Once started, you can still re-open the link to finish any remaining steps, without generating duplicate rewards.
