Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is there any joining or membership fee?

No. Creating an account on Earnova and browsing partner offers is completely free. Certain banking or trading offers may require standard account funding or a first trade as specified in that partner's terms.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

You can request a withdrawal once your Available Balance reaches ₹30 or more. Supported methods include UPI ID transfers and Bank Transfers (Account Number + IFSC).

Why does a task show as "Started ✓"?

To prevent duplicate entries, each offer reward can only be claimed once per user. You can reopen the partner link anytime, though rewards are credited only on your first qualifying completion.

How do I contact support for tracking or payout help?

You can email our support desk at rajneshc510@gmail.com with your registered email and offer name, or read our Privacy Policy.
